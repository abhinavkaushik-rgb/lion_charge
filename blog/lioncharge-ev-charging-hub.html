Have a look at the future of EV charging at our Hyderabad Experience Center

Exciting news for EV users in Hyderabad. LionCharge is setting up India’s first EV charging experience center on road no 45, jubilee hills, Hyderabad. At LionCharge EV hub users can charge at our 50kW EV charging station while sipping some tea and relaxing at our lounge while their car is fast charging. EV charging Hub consists of 5 chargers which can support all kind of four wheelers, three wheelers and two wheelers.
